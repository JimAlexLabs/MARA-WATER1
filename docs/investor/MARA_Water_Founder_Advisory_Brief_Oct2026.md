## 7. Tax & company transfer — benefits and honesty

### 7.1 Excise window
- Bottled water **not excisable from 1 July 2026** → no excise stamps on new manufacture → cost stack improves vs refill-only holding pattern.  
- Plan as if policy can change around **mid/late 2027**; hence **term ends July 2027** with renewal clause.

### 7.2 Turnover Tax (TOT) — real rules (verify with tax agent)
- TOT generally applies where annual business turnover is **above KES 1,000,000 and not more than KES 25,000,000**.  
- Rate historically **1.5% of gross turnover** (confirm current year with KRA/agent).  
- At September pace (~1.5M/month), annualised ~**18M** — **inside** the 25M band **if** you stay there.  
- Transfer of directors/shares **does not magically create TOT**; the **company’s turnover profile and KRA registration** do. Continuity of the existing private company can avoid reinventing licences and may support simpler regimes **if eligible**.  
- TOT does **not** erase: **VAT** (if threshold/registration applies), **PAYE**, **NHIF/SHIF/NSSF**, county licences, NEMA/water quality obligations, etc.

### 7.3 Benefits of clean director/share transfer (practical)
- Bank account + M-Pesa till in operators’ control.  
- Ability to hire and pay staff under current PIN.  
- Clear authority to restart sealed production.  
- Investor confidence (Derrick will not wire into Kenneth’s sole mandate).

**Action:** Appoint a **tax agent + company secretary** this week to: update directors, share transfers/allotments, beneficial ownership, KRA contacts, optional TOT position letter.
